Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Courtesy translation. If there is any difference between this text and the Portuguese version, the Portuguese version prevails.

This policy explains what data DT Nexus processes, why, who it is shared with and what your rights are, under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018). It covers this website and the DT Nexus CRM system.

1. Who is who

For the data of the Company’s account and its users (who signs up for and uses the system) and for the data of people who contact us, DT Nexus is the controller. For the data a Company enters about its own customers (name, phone, license plate, service history, messages), the Company is the controller and DT Nexus is the processor: we process that data only to provide the service, following the Company’s instructions.

Controller: Daniel dos Santos Oliveira (an individual), Brazilian taxpayer ID (CPF) 775.000.082-87, Manaus, Amazonas, Brazil.

Data protection officer and privacy contact: Daniel dos Santos Oliveira, dtpel.empreendimento@gmail.com.

2. This website

This marketing website does not use cookies, has no analytics or advertising tools and does not ask you to sign up. It does not load third-party resources: images, video and styles are served from the site itself. The contact buttons open WhatsApp (Meta) with a ready-made message; when you write to us, we process your name, phone number and the content of the conversation only to reply and to handle the trial or purchase (LGPD art. 7, V, steps preliminary to a contract). The server that delivers the site may record the IP address and the time of access for security, like any web server.

4. Why we use it (purpose) and the legal basis

  • Providing the contracted service (account, orders, finance, WhatsApp): performance of a contract (LGPD art. 7, V).
  • Charging and confirming payments: performance of a contract and legal obligation (art. 7, II and V).
  • Security, fraud and abuse prevention (login attempt limits, new-IP alerts, log of rejected webhooks): legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).
  • Proving acceptance of notices and terms (including IP and browser): regular exercise of rights (art. 7, VI) and legitimate interest.
  • Account communications (trial and subscription reminders, maintenance notices): performance of a contract.

We do not sell data and we do not use the Companies’ customer data for our own advertising.

6. International transfers

Some providers (for example Stripe, Cloudflare and Meta) may process data outside Brazil. We do this on the grounds the LGPD allows (art. 33), such as performance of the contract and adequate clauses or safeguards from the providers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Company account and business data: while the account is active. After access ends, it remains available for export for 90 days and may then be deleted, except what the law requires us to keep.
  • Files received through WhatsApp (photos, audio, documents): 90 days by default, a period the Company can adjust; after that the file is deleted and the message record remains, showing that the file was removed.
  • Acceptances and proof of notice: for the duration of the contract and the applicable limitation period.
  • Security logs: for as long as needed for the security of the service and to meet legal obligations.
  • Backups: keep copies of the data for a limited time, until replaced by newer copies.

8. Your rights

You may ask for confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymization or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about who we share it with, and withdraw consent you gave (LGPD art. 18). Ask at dtpel.empreendimento@gmail.com; we reply within a reasonable time and may ask you to confirm your identity. If you are a customer of a shop that uses DT Nexus, make the request to that shop, which is the controller of your data; we help the shop to meet it. You may also complain to Brazil’s data protection authority (ANPD).

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), separation of each Company’s data, profile-based access control, login attempt limits, 12-hour sessions, new-IP alerts for administrators and backups. No system is 100% secure. In case of an incident that may cause relevant risk, we will notify the affected Company and the ANPD as the law requires.

10. Cookies and third-party resources in the system

The system uses only necessary cookies: keeping you signed in, remembering the language you chose and protecting against attacks. We do not use advertising cookies. To display the screens, the browser loads libraries and fonts from content delivery networks (for example jsDelivr, Cloudflare and Google Fonts), which can see the IP address.

11. Children and teenagers

The service is aimed at businesses and is not intended for people under 18.
