FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. The system runs in your browser, on computer and phone. Mechanics also get their own access on their phones.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The trial lasts 14 days outside Brazil, with no card required. If you do not subscribe, your account becomes read-only and your data can still be exported.

What languages does it support?

English, Spanish and Portuguese. Each person on your team picks the language they prefer.

Is there a contract or automatic renewal?

No. The monthly plan has no lock-in, and the annual plan is a single payment for 365 days with no automatic renewal.

Does WhatsApp cost extra?

DT Nexus does not charge extra for WhatsApp. With the official Meta API, you use your own Meta business account, and Meta may charge its own fees for some message types. Check Meta's price list before you turn it on.

Is my shop's data separate from other shops?

Yes. Each shop has its own data, isolated from all others.

What support do I get?

Support runs Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm Manaus time (UTC-4). Annual plan: first reply within 2 business hours by WhatsApp. Monthly plan: first reply within 1 business day by e-mail or WhatsApp.
