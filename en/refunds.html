Cancellation & refunds

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Summary: you try it before you pay, you can cancel any time, nothing renews automatically, and if you change your mind within 7 days of paying, we refund you.

1. Free trial

The trial lasts 30 days (14 days for accounts outside Brazil), with no charge and no card required. If you do not subscribe, the account becomes read-only: your data stays visible and can be exported, and nothing is charged.

2. Cancelling

  • Monthly plan: no lock-in and no penalty. Each 30-day period is paid one at a time, so to stop you simply do not pay the next period, or tell us.
  • Annual plan: a single payment, with access for 365 days and no automatic renewal. Nothing is charged again unless you buy again.
  • Outside Brazil (card, in US dollars): the same rule: each period is bought and paid one at a time, with no automatic renewal.

After access ends, your data remains available for export for 90 days.

3. Refund within 7 days

If you paid and changed your mind within 7 calendar days of the payment, we refund the full amount, to the same payment method (card or Pix). This period follows art. 49 of the Brazilian Consumer Defense Code, and we offer it to customers outside Brazil as well.

4. After 7 days

After that period, the paid period is not refunded pro rata, and you keep access until it ends. If we made a mistake, such as a duplicate charge or a service that did not work because of the system, we look into it and fix it, with a refund when appropriate.

5. How to ask

Send your request to dtpel.empreendimento@gmail.com or by WhatsApp to +55 92 98568-5906, with the business name and the payment date. We reply within 1 business day (Manaus time, UTC-4).

6. How long the refund takes

Once approved, we ask the payment processor (Stripe or Asaas) to reverse the payment. The money shows up depending on the method: for cards, usually on the next statement or the one after, depending on the issuing bank.

7. A charge you do not recognize

If a charge appears that you did not make, contact us before opening a dispute with your bank: we handle it with priority and reverse whatever is not valid.
